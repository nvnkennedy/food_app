Virundhu – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Virundhu is a family meal planner. It is designed so that your data stays on your device.

What the app stores

Names, ages, height, weight and goals of the family members you enter; the meals you swap, mark as eaten or as favourites; your grocery ticks; your weight log; your country, weekly budget and theme. All of this is stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There is no account, no login and no analytics.

Sharing

If you use "Share setup link" or "Share grocery list", the data is encoded into a link or text that you choose to send through your own messaging app. We do not receive it.

Network

The web version loads from GitHub Pages and the Inter font from Google Fonts. These services see your IP address as part of normal web hosting. The Android app bundles everything and works offline.

Children

The app lets a parent enter a child's name and age to size portions. This information never leaves the device.

Deleting your data

Remove a profile inside the app, or uninstall the app / clear the site data in your browser. Everything is deleted with it.

Health disclaimer

Calorie and nutrient figures are estimates based on typical ingredients and standard reference intakes. They are not medical advice. Consult a doctor or dietitian for personal health decisions.
